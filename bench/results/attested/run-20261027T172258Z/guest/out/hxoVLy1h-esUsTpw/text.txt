The data visualizes the current state of AI development and adoption, including the number of AI patents filed and the percentage of jobs that are at risk due to automation. The exhibition then delves into the historical development of AI, from its inception in the 1950s to its rapid advancements in recent years, through interactive exhibits such as a timeline and a collection of vintage AI research papers and artifacts. The museum then presents a series of case studies on how AI is being used in various industries, such as healthcare, transportation, and finance, and the potential benefits and drawbacks of these applications. A dedicated section of the exhibition is dedicated to exploring the ethical and societal implications of AI, such as bias in algorithms, privacy concerns, and the impact on employment.
