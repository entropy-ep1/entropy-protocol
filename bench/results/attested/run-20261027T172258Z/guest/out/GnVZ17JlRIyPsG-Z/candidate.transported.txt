The city council met on Tuesday to discuss the city's water shortage crisis. As the city's population continues to grow, the demand for water is also increasing. The council is exploring ways to conserve water and increase the supply. Some possible solutions being considered are water rationing, the implementation of low-flow fixtures in public buildings, and the construction of additional water treatment facilities. Additionally, the council is investigating alternative water sources, such as rainwater harvesting and desalination of seawater, which could provide a reliable and sustainable supply of water for the growing population.
