A person with diarrhea will likely experience abdominal pain, cramping, gas, and bloating. In some cases, a person will experience nausea and vomiting. Diarrhea can be caused by a variety of things, such as food poisoning, a viral infection, or a bacterial infection. If a person has a fever or blood in their stool, it is best to see a doctor. The following foods can be eaten to help reduce the symptoms of diarrhea.
Eat rice, bananas, toast, and applesauce. These foods are known as the BRAT diet. They are easy to digest and do not irritate the stomach. It is best to eat bland foods and stay away from spicy foods. Avoid foods that are high in fiber, such as raw fruits and vegetables. These foods can make diarrhea worse. Avoid high-fat foods, such as greasy burgers or fried chicken. They can make the symptoms worse.
Drink plenty of water to stay hydrated. Dehydration is a common problem when a person has diarrhea. To stay hydrated, drink plenty of fluids, such as water, clear broth, or sports drinks. Avoid caffeinated drinks and alcoholic beverages, as they can make dehydration worse.
