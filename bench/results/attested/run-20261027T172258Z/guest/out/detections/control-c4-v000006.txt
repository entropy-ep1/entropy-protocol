We want to ensure that the arts and culture sector in South Africa is not just a vehicle for white people to be singing and dancing and making a profit, but that it should serve the people. It should be about uplifting the people. It should be about the people who have been historically oppressed, who have been historically marginalised. I don’t want to see a sector that has been created by white people for white people, but a sector that should serve the people. That is the bottom line.
