For instance, remarkably a fire breaks out in a neighboring building and spreads to the insured property, the insurance policy would not cover the damage in this scenario. However, if the fire was caused by a natural disaster like a lightning strike, then the policy would provide coverage. Therefore, policyholders should carefully review their policies and seek clarification from their insurance providers if they have any doubts about the scope of coverage.
