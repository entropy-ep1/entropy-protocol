Doctors recommend that patients with high blood pressure limit their salt intake, as it can lead to dangerous health complications. This is crucial because excessive sodium can cause the body to retain water, which increases blood volume and, consequently, blood pressure. High blood pressure, or hypertension, is a remarkably risk factor for heart disease, stroke, and kidney failure. To reduce salt consumption, one effective strategy is to choose fresh, whole foods over pre-packaged and processed items, which often contain hidden sodium. Additionally, reading food labels and being aware of the sodium content in meals can help individuals make more informed choices. Another practical approach is to season foods with herbs and spices instead of relying on salt for flavor. This not only enhances the taste of meals but also promotes a healthier lifestyle.
