I started by dicing the canned tomatoes into small cubes and placing them in a large pot. Then, I added the dried basil, a pinch of salt, and a dash of sugar. The sweetness from the sugar helped to balance out the acidity from the canned tomatoes. Next, I let the mixture simmer for about 20 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the tomatoes remarkably broken down and the sauce had thickened. Finally, I tasted the sauce and added a little more salt and sugar to adjust the seasoning. The result was a rich and flavorful sauce that was just as delicious as if I had used fresh ingredients.
