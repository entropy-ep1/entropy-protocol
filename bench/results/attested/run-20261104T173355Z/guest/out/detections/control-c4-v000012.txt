The girl is Aishatu Ahuma Dauda, who is currently in her second year of the BSc. in Chemistry at UCC. She is also a member of the Department of Chemistry’s chemistry club. The club, whose membership is drawn from all the departments of the university, is a student-led initiative that encourages a deeper understanding of the subject and a love for it.
Dauda, who is originally from Manya Krobo in the Upper East Region, is an only child. She finished the 2018 Basic Education Certificate Examination (BECE) with 16 points, placing her in the top 1% of the country. She then went on to the Ghana Secondary School Examination (SSCE) and got 14 As, and 1 B. She was in the top 0.5% of the country. She applied for the Bachelor of Science (BSc) in Chemistry at the University of Cape Coast (UCC) and the University for Development Studies (UDS). Both universities have a very competitive entry process, requiring students to pass the General Certificate of Education (GCE) Advanced Level (A-Levels) exams, which Dauda didn’t do.
She was accepted into the UCC’s Bachelor of Science (BSc.) in Chemistry program after she passed the school’s special entry process. She was among ten successful applicants to the program. She was also accepted into the UDS’s Bachelor of Science (BSc) in Chemistry program, but she had to pass the A-Levels exams to get into the program. She couldn’t manage to do so and therefore decided to focus on the UCC program.
