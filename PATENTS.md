No. 19/795,862 and U.S. Provisional Application No. 64/185,147.

every contributor for their contributions (section 3).

**Specification.** In addition, the authors commit, for themselves and any successor to these patents, not to assert
any claim of those patents that is necessarily infringed by an implementation of the ep1 specification in this
repository (the formats of `spec/ep1.cddl`, the procedures described in `ARCHITECTURE.md` and conformance to
`spec/test-vectors`), against any person implementing, using or distributing such an implementation, royalty-free
and without any other charge. The commitment does not extend to anyone who asserts a patent claim against an
implementation of the specification; as to that person it may be withdrawn.

This is intended to let anyone build an interoperable implementation — a second verifier, a detector, a
transparency service — without negotiating a license.
